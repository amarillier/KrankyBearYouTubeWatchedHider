YouTube Watched Hider

Hide what you've already watched, and keep a short list of the channels you actually follow.

Quick start

  1. Browse YouTube as normal. Videos you've watched are hidden automatically.
  2. Use the red/grey button at the bottom-left of YouTube, or the toolbar icon, to turn hiding on or off.
  3. Open a channel you care about and click + Track channel (bottom-right). It then appears on your Subscriptions page.

Hiding watched videos

It works on Home, Subscriptions, channel pages, search results and the watch-page sidebar. It never hides anything on your History page.

The bottom-left button shows the current state and counts, for example Hiding watched · 12 hidden · 8 shown. Counts cover the videos loaded so far on that page, so they grow as you scroll. Click it to switch hiding on or off.

A video counts as watched when its progress reaches the threshold in the toolbar popup (default 10%). Progress comes from:

  • YouTube's own red progress bar on thumbnails, which follows your account across devices.
  • Your own playback on watch pages and Shorts (ads and hover previews are ignored).
  • Videos you mark yourself (below), and anything imported from a backup or Google Takeout.

Once the extension has seen a video's progress it remembers it, even if YouTube later stops showing the bar.

Marking a video as watched

Hover over a thumbnail and click ✓ Watched. The video is stored as fully watched and hides straight away if hiding is on. An Undo link appears for a few seconds. This is useful for videos YouTube shows as only 15–20% watched even though you finished them.

If a video was counted as watched by mistake, turn hiding off, hover over it and click ✗ Unwatch. This overrides YouTube's own progress bar for that video until you play it again.

Hiding Shorts and Releases

Two optional switches in the popup:

  • Hide Shorts removes Shorts shelves, Shorts tiles, the Shorts channel tab and the Shorts entry in the side menu. It applies even when hiding of watched videos is off, but never on your History page.
  • Hide channel Releases removes the Releases tab and the Releases shelf on channel pages.

Tabs and shelves are found by their English label, so these two switches only work with YouTube set to English.

Keyboard shortcut

Press Alt+Shift+H to turn hiding of watched videos on or off. To change it, open chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Tracked channels

Click + Track channel on any channel page to add it, and click it again to remove it. Your normal YouTube subscriptions are not changed.

On the Subscriptions page (and optionally the Home page) a strip appears at the top with one chip per tracked channel and the number of unwatched videos. Click a chip to open that channel's Videos tab, where hiding already applies. The ↻ button refreshes the counts.

The same list, with counts and links, is in the toolbar popup.

About the counts: they are based on each channel's 15 most recent uploads. A badge such as 15+ means all of those 15 are unwatched and there may be more. Counts refresh at most every 15 minutes.

Shorts: a channel's feed mixes Shorts in with normal videos, but Shorts never appear on its Videos tab. So by default they are not counted. Tick Count Shorts in channel totals in the popup to include them.

Which videos are counted? In the popup, click a channel's number badge to expand a list of the unwatched videos behind it, with titles, dates and links.

Sorting and ordering

In the popup, choose Manual, A – Z, Z – A or Most unwatched. The popup list and the YouTube strip both follow your choice. To set your own order, drag the ☰ handle on a row. Dragging switches to Manual and keeps the order you see.

Backup, moving to another computer

Export backup saves your settings, tracked channels and watched videos to one JSON file. Import… on another computer merges it in: watched videos keep the higher progress, missing channels are added, and the settings in the file are applied.

Import also accepts a Google Takeout watch-history.json (Takeout → YouTube → History, JSON format) to mark everything you've ever watched. Clear memory forgets the remembered videos, though YouTube's own progress bars are still detected.

Troubleshooting

  • Nothing hides: check the toggle is on, then refresh the YouTube tab. YouTube changes its page layout from time to time, which can break the tile detection. If the bottom-left button turns orange and says it can't find videos, that is what has happened.
  • Counts look too high: videos you watched elsewhere only count once the extension has seen their progress. Import Takeout, or open the channel page once. Click the channel's badge in the popup to see exactly which videos are counted. Live streams and premieres can also be counted even though they aren't on the Videos tab.
  • The + button doesn't appear: it shows only on a channel's own page. Give it a second after the page loads.
  • After updating the extension: reload it on chrome://extensions and refresh your YouTube tabs.

Privacy

Everything is stored locally in your browser. The extension reads YouTube pages you visit and fetches the public RSS feed of each tracked channel from youtube.com. It does not use an API key, has no server, and sends your data nowhere.
